CSM AureumThe Event

One day, two halves

By day

Five modules that mix fun with real learning. Delegates investigate, pitch, reason, negotiate and perform, and collect points as they go.

By night

A social evening, planned as a concert, where all delegates come together after a full day of competition.

ModuleDay programme

Crime and Investigation

Delegates are handed a mystery and a trail of clues. The task is to piece it together and reach a verdict, or solve the case outright.

Round 1

A mystery is issued and teams work through the clues to reach a conclusion.

Round 2

A second case for the teams that move forward, where sharper reasoning decides the outcome.

ModuleDay programme

Shark Tank

Teams receive a theme, a short clock and limited resources. They build a business model and present it to a panel that awards points.

Round 1

Prepare and pitch a business model to the panel. The highest scoring teams are shortlisted.

Round 2

The same format with even less time and fewer resources, against tougher competition.

ModuleDay programme

The Galileo Summit

A test of knowledge and nerve, where every choice carries a risk.

Round 1

Choose between easy questions worth fewer points or difficult ones that pay more.

Round 2

Guess the word from one-word hints. Some wrong guesses will cost you points.

ModuleDay programme

The Keynesian Gambit

Economics meets invention. Delegates sell, bid and build.

Round 1

Pitch a randomly assigned item to a panel of judges.

Round 2

Bid in an auction for technologies and materials, then create an invention from what you win.

ModuleDay programme

Pangea's Ecosystem

Delegates act as decision makers who have to do the most good with the least.

Round 1

Solve a series of crises using limited resources and a fixed budget.

Round 2

Allocate a limited budget to aid a disaster-hit region.

PointsDay programme

The Shop

Earn

Every score in every module turns into points for the delegation.

Spend

Points buy perks that help in other modules, or even a re-entry into an ongoing module a delegate has already completed.

Check

Each delegation's balance can be viewed and used in the designated shop room.

ModuleDay programme

Talent Show

A performance, a monologue, a song or anything else. Participants prepare their own act and present it to a panel.

Round 1

The panel watches every act and shortlists those who move on.

Round 2

The shortlisted performers take the stage again and a winner is chosen.

AlongsideDay programme

Side Quests

Being eliminated does not end the day. Delegates who exit early can join the e-gaming side quests, win points there and exchange them for refreshments.

EveningSocial night

Social Night

When the competition ends, the evening begins. The day closes with a social night, planned as a concert, so every delegate leaves with a shared memory.

LeadershipRoles

Advisors and President

Executive Advisors

  • Supervise every module and department from planning to the final day
  • Guide the President on major decisions and step in on escalated issues
  • Report to school management on progress, budget and conduct
  • Keep the event in line with school policy, safety and its educational purpose

President

  • Leads the organising body and sets the vision for CSM Aureum
  • Approves major plans, schedules and partnerships
  • Works with the CFO and COO to keep every department aligned
  • Resolves conflicts and keeps the team accountable and motivated
LeadershipRoles

Finance and Operations

Chief Finance Officer

  • Prepares the overall budget and the allocation for each department
  • Analyses spending and reviews every department's financial reports
  • Approves expenses and tracks sponsorship income
  • Flags overspending early and reports to the President and advisors

Chief Operating Officer

  • Coordinates all departments so work never overlaps or stalls
  • Owns the master timeline and the schedule for the event day
  • Meets directors regularly to check progress and remove blockers
  • Resolves cross-department issues and keeps modules running smoothly
DepartmentsRoles

How each department works

Director

  • Owns the department's goals, plans and results
  • Assigns tasks and sets deadlines
  • Reports to the COO, and to the CFO on money matters
  • Represents the department in leadership meetings

Co-Director

  • Supports the Director and steps in when they are away
  • Manages daily work and follows up with the team
  • Checks the quality of work before it is submitted
  • Acts as the link between the Director and members

Team Members

  • Four to ten per department, depending on workload
  • Carry out assigned tasks to the agreed standard
  • Update the Co-Director on progress and challenges
  • Support other departments where needed on the day
DepartmentsRoles

Department duties, part one

Finance and Logistics

  • Draft the department budget with the CFO and record every income and expense
  • Arrange equipment, materials, transport and venue setup
  • Manage supplies for the modules, the shop and the side quests

External Relations

  • Identify and approach sponsors and partners
  • Handle agreements and communication with outside organisations
  • Deliver what was promised to sponsors and thank them afterwards

Hospitality

  • Welcome and register delegates, guests and judges
  • Look after refreshments, seating and the help desk
  • Handle internal affairs, including volunteer schedules and welfare
DepartmentsRoles

Department duties, part two

Decor

  • Design the venue theme for each module and the social night
  • Build the stage and concert backdrop within budget
  • Source materials, set up in time and clear up afterwards

Media and Graphics

  • Create the logo, posters and social media graphics
  • Photograph and film the day and share live updates
  • Prepare slides, certificates and post event content

Marketing and Outreach

  • Promote CSM Aureum to students and other schools
  • Run registrations and delegation sign ups
  • Manage social media campaigns and public announcements
